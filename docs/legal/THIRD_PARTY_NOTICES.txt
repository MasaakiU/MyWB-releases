RF-DETR
-------

The band detection model in this distribution was fine-tuned from the
RF-DETR Small pretrained weights (https://github.com/roboflow/rf-detr),
Copyright 2025 Roboflow, Inc., licensed under the Apache License 2.0. The
weights were further trained on gel images to detect bands and exported to
ONNX. The rfdetr package itself is not included in this distribution.

License text:

third_party/rfdetr/LICENSE

GelGenie Dataset
----------------

The band detection model in this distribution was trained in part on
"Segmented Gel Electrophoresis Images used for Training GelGenie Models" by
Matthew Aquilina et al. (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14641893), licensed
under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License. For
training, its segmentation masks were converted to bounding boxes and
corrected by hand, and its images were augmented. Neither the dataset nor any
GelGenie model or code is included in this distribution.
