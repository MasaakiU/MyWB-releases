# Troubleshooting

[← Back to MyWB](../README.md)

## The app does not open

On macOS, follow the [first-launch instructions](installation.md#first-launch). If `Open Anyway` is not visible, try to open `MyWB.app` once more; the button only appears for about one hour after a blocked launch attempt. On a Mac managed by an organization, contact your administrator if the setting is unavailable.

## The app cannot access files

Make sure MyWB has permission to access the folder containing your images or output files. On macOS, folder access can be managed in `System Settings` > `Privacy & Security`.

## Report a bug or ask a question

Questions about using MyWB are welcome, not only bug reports. Check the existing threads on [MyWB GitHub Issues](https://github.com/MasaakiU/MyWB-releases/issues) and open a new issue if you don't find an answer.

For bug reports, please include:

- your MyWB version;
- your operating system and version;
- the steps needed to reproduce the problem;
- what you expected to happen and what happened instead; and
- the exact error message or a screenshot, when available.

Do not include confidential research data, credentials, or personal information in the report.
