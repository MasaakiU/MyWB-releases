# Troubleshooting

[← Back to README](../README.md)

## The app does not open

On macOS, follow the [first-launch instructions](installation.md#first-launch). If `Open Anyway` is not visible, try to open `MyWB.app` once more; the button only appears for about one hour after a blocked launch attempt. On a Mac managed by an organization, contact your administrator if the setting is unavailable.

## The app cannot access files

Make sure MyWB has permission to access the folder containing your images or output files. On macOS, folder access can be managed in `System Settings` > `Privacy & Security`.
